!1 ER Diagram — Cross-Channel Bridge

The '''only bridge''' between iMEP (Email) and SharePoint (Intranet). Runs via '''TrackingId ↔ __ORG__GICTrackingID''' at the dimension level (tbl_email ↔ pages), '''never''' directly via engagement facts. Plus the employee bridge via tbl_hr_employee.WORKER_ID.

!2 The Two Bridges

!3 Bridge 1: TrackingID (Campaign-Level)

'''Where?''': tbl_email.TrackingId ↔ sharepoint_bronze.pages.__ORG__GICTrackingID
'''Grain''': Pack (Cluster + Pack-Number), optionally Activity (SEG1-3).

Match logic:
{{{
-- Match at Pack level (SEG1-2). SEG3=date, SEG4=activity-seq and SEG5=channel all differ
-- between email and the matching intranet page of the same pack.
ON  array_join(slice(split(UPPER(email.TrackingId),        '-'), 1, 2), '-')
  = array_join(slice(split(UPPER(pages.__ORG__GICTrackingID), '-'), 1, 2), '-')
}}}

Coverage reality:
 * iMEP side: a small minority of mailings (low single-digit percent) have TrackingId
 * SP side: a small minority of pages (low single-digit percent) have TrackingID
 * '''Realistic pack intersection''': a few dozen packs — the dashboard universe

!3 Bridge 2: Employee Identity (Person-Level)

'''Where?''': tbl_hr_employee.WORKER_ID (iMEP) ↔ sharepoint_bronze.pageviews.user_gpn (SharePoint)

Both are GPN in the format 00100200 (8-digit). WORKER_ID in HR, user_gpn in SP Bronze.

Alternative (not yet validated): sharepoint_gold.pbi_db_employeecontact carries T_NUMBER directly — could be a shorter path than the GPN detour.

!2 Why Not a Direct Engagement Join?

Common misconception: "Can I join tbl_analytics_link.TNumber directly with something in SharePoint to see if the same person opened the email AND read the page?"

Answer: Not directly, because:
 * SharePoint does not carry a TNumber — only user_gpn or viewingcontactid
 * Engagement tables carry no TrackingID in either domain

The correct path is a 5-hop chain:

{{{
tbl_analytics_link.TNumber
        |  JOIN tbl_hr_employee.T_NUMBER
        v
tbl_hr_employee.WORKER_ID  (= GPN)
        |  JOIN sharepoint_bronze.pageviews.user_gpn
        v
pageviews.pageId
        |  JOIN sharepoint_bronze.pages.pageUUID
        v
pages.__ORG__GICTrackingID  (if present)
        |  SEG1-2 compare (Pack-Level)
        v
tbl_email.TrackingId
}}}

In practice you don't do this row-by-row but aggregated at pack level — see !see .EmployeeEngagement.CPlanGICTrackingCLARITYDashboard.MultiChannelDataModel.JoinStrategy.CrossChannelViaTrackingId.
